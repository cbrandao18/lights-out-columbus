About this app

Each record is sent to the same place as the Lights Out Columbus v2.0 survey in Survey123. Records and photos are kept on this phone until they are sent, so you can fill one in without signal and send it later. If a photo fails to upload, only that photo is retried; the bird is never entered twice.

Finding a bird

Log every window-collision bird or bat: dead, live, or one that got away. One record per bird.

Live birds

Write the unique ID, date, time and address on the bag. That helps with admission at the hospital. The shift's transport person takes them to the Ohio Wildlife Center Rehabilitation Clinic or the Grange Insurance Audubon Center.

Ohio Wildlife Center Grange Audubon Center

Dead birds

Write the unique ID and date on the bag or specimen tag.

Injured Rock Pigeons

City Bird Rehab accepts live, injured Rock Pigeons found downtown. Message them on Facebook first. They also take porch drop-offs, but send a message so they know it's there.

3105 Norwood St, Columbus, OH 43224

City Bird Rehab on Facebook Apple Maps Google Maps

Ohio Wildlife Center does not rehabilitate non-native species like Rock Pigeons, but will accept them for humane euthanasia.
